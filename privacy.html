Hub Universe privacy policy

Hub Universe is an app by Marvel Corp, part of the Hub family (Day Hub, Money Hub, Home Hub, Auto Hub, Trip Hub, Cruise Hub, Health Hub). Last updated October 22, 2026.

The short version

Hub Universe shows your other Hub apps on one screen. It reads what those apps already saved on your phone, on your phone, and never changes it or sends it anywhere. We have no Hub Universe account system and no database of your information. There are no ads, no ad trackers and no analytics in the app. A few features you choose to turn on send data to other services, and each one is listed below.

What stays on your phone

Your other hubs' data. To show what needs you, Hub Universe reads the data your other Hub apps saved in this browser's storage on your phone (for example bills due in Money Hub, service due in Auto Hub, doses due in Health Hub, upcoming trips in Trip Hub or Cruise Hub). It reads it on the phone only, for a moment, to work out the summary on screen. It never changes another hub's data, never saves a copy of it, and never sends it off the phone.

Hub Universe's own settings (your first name, the weather city, background and display choices, reminders and anything you add in Hub Universe itself) are saved in your browser's storage on your phone. We cannot see them. You can erase them any time in ⚙ Settings → Your data → Erase everything. Erase also signs the phone out of Google, tells Google to drop that sign-in, and forgets saved purchase emails and the AI passphrase for every Hub app on the phone. It does not erase your other hubs' own data; each hub has its own Erase. Uninstalling the app or clearing its site data also removes Hub Universe's data.

Google user data

Hub Universe's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide the features you turned on, in the app, on your phone. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to train AI models, and no person at Marvel Corp reads it. To disconnect, use ⚙ Settings in the app or remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The backup copy can be deleted from Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps.

Children

Hub Universe is for people 13 and older. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Selling data

We do not sell or rent personal information. We hold almost none of it.

Changes

If what Hub Universe sends anywhere changes, this page changes first, and the date at the top changes with it.
